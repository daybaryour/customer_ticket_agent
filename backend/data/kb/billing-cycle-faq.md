# Billing Cycle FAQ

**Product:** Acme
**Category:** Billing
**Last updated:** 2024-12-10

## When does my billing cycle start?

Your billing cycle starts on the day you **first subscribed** and renews on the same day each billing period. For example, if you subscribed on March 15 with a monthly plan, you'll be billed on the **15th of every month**.

## Where can I see my next billing date?

1. Go to **Settings → Billing**.
2. The **Next payment** section shows the date and amount.
3. Click **View invoice history** for past invoices.

## What payment methods does Acme accept?

- Visa
- Mastercard
- American Express
- PayPal (annual plans only)
- Wire transfer (annual plans over $5,000 — contact **billing@acme.io**)

## What happens if my payment fails?

1. **Day 0**: We retry the charge immediately.
2. **Day 3**: Second retry. You'll receive an email notification.
3. **Day 5**: Third retry.
4. **Day 7**: If payment still fails, your account is **downgraded to the Free plan** and seats beyond the free limit are deactivated.

Update your payment method anytime at **Settings → Billing → Payment method**.

## How do I change from monthly to annual billing?

1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Plan**.
2. Click **Switch to annual**.
3. The change takes effect **immediately** (for upgrades) or at the **next renewal date** (for downgrades).
4. Unused days from your current monthly cycle are **prorated as credit** toward the annual charge.

## How do I pause my subscription?

Acme allows a **one-time pause** of up to **3 months** per calendar year:

1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Pause subscription**.
2. Choose a pause duration (1, 2, or 3 months).
3. Your account switches to a read-only state during the pause.
4. Billing resumes automatically at the end of the pause period.

## Can I get an invoice for my company records?

Yes. Invoices are available under **Settings → Billing → Invoice history**. You can also **edit your billing details** (company name, tax ID, address) at **Settings → Billing → Billing details** to ensure invoices are issued correctly.

## Tax and VAT

Acme collects sales tax, VAT, or GST where required by law based on your billing address. The tax amount is itemized on every invoice.

## Pro-rated upgrades

If you add seats or upgrade tiers mid-cycle, you're charged a **pro-rated amount** for the remainder of the current cycle. The new total appears immediately in your billing summary.
