# Password Reset FAQ

**Product:** Acme
**Category:** Account & Login
**Last updated:** 2024-11-15

## How do I reset my password?

1. Go to **https://app.acme.io/login**
2. Click the **Forgot password?** link below the sign-in form.
3. Enter the email address associated with your Acme account.
4. Check your inbox for a message from **no-reply@acme.io** with the subject "Reset your Acme password."
5. Click the **Reset Password** button in the email. The link expires after **30 minutes**.
6. Enter a new password that meets our requirements and click **Save**.

## Password requirements

Your new password must:

- Be at least **12 characters** long
- Contain at least one uppercase letter
- Contain at least one lowercase letter
- Contain at least one number
- Contain at least one special character (e.g., `!`, `@`, `#`, `$`)
- Not match any of your previous **4 passwords**

## I didn't receive the reset email. What should I do?

- Check your **spam/junk** folder.
- Wait up to **5 minutes** — delivery can occasionally be delayed.
- Verify you're checking the same email address tied to your account.
- Add **no-reply@acme.io** to your safe senders list and request a new link.

If the issue persists after 15 minutes, contact support via the in-app chat or email **support@acme.io**.

## The reset link expired. What now?

The reset token expires after **30 minutes**. Simply request a new link from the login page — the old token becomes invalid immediately.

## Can I reset my password from the mobile app?

Yes. Open the Acme mobile app, tap **Sign In → Forgot Password**, and follow the same steps. The reset flow is identical on web and mobile.

## I'm locked out of my account. How do I regain access?

Accounts are temporarily locked after **10 failed login attempts** for **15 minutes**. Wait for the lock to expire, then request a password reset. If the lock persists or you suspect unauthorized access, contact **security@acme.io**.

## 2FA and password resets

If you have two-factor authentication (2FA) enabled, you'll be prompted for your 2FA code after resetting your password. If you've also lost access to your 2FA device, you'll need to complete the **account recovery process**, which requires government-issued ID verification and takes 1–2 business days.
