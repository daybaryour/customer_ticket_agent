# Refund Policy

**Product:** Acme
**Category:** Billing
**Last updated:** 2024-12-01

## Overview

Acme offers refunds under the following conditions. Refunds are issued to the **original payment method** whenever possible and typically appear within **5–10 business days** after approval.

## Eligibility

### Monthly subscriptions

- **First-time purchasers**: We offer a **14-day money-back guarantee** from the date of the initial purchase.
- **Renewals**: No refunds are issued for monthly renewal charges once the new billing cycle has begun.

### Annual subscriptions

- **First-time purchasers**: The **30-day money-back guarantee** applies.
- **Renewals**: A **prorated refund** is available if requested within **14 days** of the renewal date. After 14 days, annual renewals are non-refundable.

### Add-ons and usage-based charges

- **Seat add-ons**: Follow the same refund rules as the subscription they were attached to.
- **Usage-based charges** (e.g., API overage, SMS): Non-refundable once billed.

## Non-refundable items

- Setup or onboarding fees
- One-time professional services or training fees
- Domain purchases or third-party pass-through charges

## How to request a refund

1. Open **Settings → Billing** in your Acme dashboard.
2. Click **Request a refund**.
3. Select the charge and provide a brief reason.
4. Submit the form. Our billing team reviews requests within **3 business days**.

You can also email **billing@acme.io** with the subject "Refund Request — [Your Account Name]" and include the invoice number.

## Downgrade vs. refund

If you no longer need a paid plan, consider **downgrading to the Free plan** instead of canceling. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period, and you retain access until then.

## Canceled accounts and refunds

Canceling your account does **not** automatically trigger a refund. You must submit a refund request separately as described above.

## Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy may be granted in cases of:

- Verified billing errors (e.g., duplicate charges)
- Service outages lasting more than **24 consecutive hours**
- Legal requirements in your jurisdiction

Contact **billing@acme.io** if you believe your situation qualifies for an exception.
